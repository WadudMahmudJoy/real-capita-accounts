# Salary payment preparation and proposed persistence (002E-1)

## Implemented and verified from existing source

- `PayrollRun` approval freezes money and employee-global snapshots; it neither authorizes payment nor posts an accrual. `PayrollRunLine.bankPay` and `cashPay` are planned allocations, with bank allocation based on configured gross percentage and cash receiving the remaining net. They contain no paid/reserved balances or disbursement identity.
- `PayrollPaymentPreparationService.prepare` and `GET /payroll-runs/:id/payment-preparation?channel=BANK|CASH` require authenticated ACCOUNTANT, active PRIMARY ownership and APPROVED status. They reuse the document financial/snapshot validator and inspect historical payment-profile references, approval/revision timestamps, gross-percentage reconciliation and required bank destination evidence inside one RepeatableRead view. No current salary configuration is substituted. Exact Decimal strings and safe employee identity fields are returned; credentials and raw snapshots remain server-side. The existing 1,000-employee cap is retained.
- The UI permits local employee selection and shows exact selected/unselected planned totals. Zero allocations remain visible and are not selectable. No balance, reservation, paid status, actual payment, voucher or audit mutation is created. Local selections confer no partial-payment authority.
- `CashBankAccount` and `LedgerAccount` in `prisma/schema.prisma` are shared/global, with no company association. Safe source candidates expose only ID, display name and ledger code. Proposed sources must exist, match BANK/CASH, be active and link to an active cash/bank ledger. Even valid candidates are rejected with `SOURCE_ACCOUNT_COMPANY_AUTHORITY_UNVERIFIABLE`, because registry presence does not prove parent ownership or authorized use. MFS is excluded. Options are capped at 200; oversized option lists fail explicitly.
- `CashBankService.ensureCashBankLedgerAccount` checks cash/bank linkage; `VoucherService.validatePostingLine` additionally checks active ledgers/fund accounts and matching ledger IDs. `VoucherService.resolveDateContext` derives company from the fiscal year and validates period membership and date ranges. `validatePostingDateContext` requires active, unclosed fiscal year and OPEN period for posting. These rules remain unchanged and are reusable; selected office must not become payroll/payment ownership.
- Existing roles/guards authorize ACCOUNTANT. Payroll approval's maker-checker contract applies to payroll approval; it is not evidence of payment authorization. Existing AuditEvent alone is not durable payment history: actor/metadata are nullable and actor deletion can detach identity, as documented in the correction preflight.

## Proposed contract requiring review before 002E-2

1. **Source authority:** approve explicit PRIMARY use of the global fund registry, or approve a persisted company/account authorization association. Preserve global accounting boundaries. Never infer ownership from names, active office, a hardcoded ID or free-text assertion. Confirm applicable currency/account controls; current CashBankAccount has no currency field or fund-reservation authority.
2. **Batching:** conservative initial option: one complete positive allocation batch per run/channel, with immutable employee amounts and no partial employee payments. Alternative: multiple batches selecting whole employee/channel allocations; or partial payments with explicit amount/balance rules. Local selection UI is only exploratory until this choice is approved. No bank-to-cash reallocation should be inferred.
3. **Persistence:** proposed `PayrollPaymentBatch` references PRIMARY, approved run/version, BANK/CASH channel, authorized real source account, exact totals, authenticated maker and eventual authorizer, version and immutable evidence. `PayrollPaymentLine` references the exact approved payroll line/global employee, planned instruction and eventual confirmed Decimal(18,2) amount. A durable append-only confirmation/evidence record stores authenticated actor, actual date, external/reference evidence and idempotency identity; approval, confirmation and accounting linkage are separate facts. Lifecycle names and transitions require approval. Restricted foreign keys retain identities and source references. No models/migrations are added here.
4. **Duplicate/excess prevention:** define an idempotency key and database uniqueness per confirmation. In Serializable transactions lock each employee/channel obligation in stable order, sum confirmed and authorized reservations using Decimal, and reject amounts above the approved allocation. Under the single-full-payment option also enforce unique employee/channel consumption. Partial/multiple payments require a reviewed reservation, cancellation/reversal and aggregate-balance contract; a batch unique key alone cannot prevent overpayment. Approved payroll lines remain unchanged. Failures must roll back instructions, confirmations, balances and audit together.
5. **Evidence/security:** keep destination instructions from approved historical profiles in restricted server-side storage. Decide the review procedure for subsequently invalid bank destinations; do not silently replace them with current profiles. Never expose full banking credentials in general payroll/preparation DTOs or audit summaries. Review evidence storage, retention, encryption/key management and narrowly authorized retrieval before implementing actual confirmations or exports. Free-text claims are not bank confirmation authority.
6. **Permissions:** explicitly authorize payment preparation, instruction authorization and disbursement confirmation using existing identities/roles; no new role is proposed. Recommend separate maker/authorizer and material-reviser exclusion, but payment maker-checker policy is not yet approved. Proposed audit events cover instruction preparation/authorization, confirmation, conflict and separately authorized corrections; immutable confirmation evidence cannot rely solely on generic mutable/nullable audit metadata.
7. **Accounting:** decide whether and when salary expense/payable accrual is recognized, the exact expense/payable/deduction ledgers, posting date, PRIMARY fiscal year/period, and permitted project/cost-center tagging. Do not infer debit salary expense versus debit salary payable from net salary. Future BANK/CASH payment vouchers must reconcile confirmed employee amounts to the fund credit and the approved accounting debit; allocations are not voucher confirmations. Require durable unique payment/voucher linkage and atomic posting or reviewed reconciliation when confirmation/posting occur separately. Approval, payment and voucher posting must not be conflated. No ledger IDs, formulas or postings are invented here.

## Acceptance boundary

Focused offline service, HTTP guard/query, exact-selection/rendering and existing document/payroll regressions verify this read-only foundation. No database connection is required or made by these checks. Existing PostgreSQL harness provider registration is maintained without executing its fixture writes. Actual disbursement is BLOCKED pending source authority, batching/partial-payment choices, authorization/evidence and reviewed persistence. Accounting posting additionally requires its own approved contract. Task 002E-2 requires separate model/migration approval before transactional recording.

## Implemented Task 002E-2 (supersedes the pending A–D policy choices above)

Policies A–D are approved: explicit PRIMARY grants, multiple employee-disjoint batches, full employee/channel allocations only, different ACCOUNTANT maker/confirmer, and independently verified BANK transaction or signed CASH acknowledgment evidence. Accounting posting remains separate.

New additive models: `PayrollSourceAuthorization`, `PayrollPaymentBatch`, `PayrollPaymentLine`, `PayrollPaymentConfirmation`. The forward migration is `20261014000000_payroll_payment_foundation`; it must not be applied to salary-dev until reviewed and separately authorized. Grants store immutable issuer/company/source/channel/time/optional expiry and audit metadata. No grant endpoint or business grant is created. A verified grant-provisioning authority/permission contract is still required for business deployment; isolated acceptance grants are relationship fixtures, not business authorizations.

`POST /payroll-runs/payment-batches` uses the write-capable authentication context and server-side maker identity. `PayrollPaymentBatchService.prepare` rechecks current active ACCOUNTANT, active PRIMARY, approved historical source/profile integrity, proposed source activity/channel/ledger, a persisted unexpired explicit grant, selected global employees and positive full allocations in Serializable isolation. It calculates amounts itself and rejects client amounts/creator/grantor fields. Batch, immutable line/obligation snapshots, durable embedded creation audit and generic audit event commit atomically. Exact replay returns the same safe DTO; a key reused for a different actor/request is a conflict. Missing reviewed tables return a controlled unavailable response.

Database enforcement includes 13 restrictive payment FKs; globally unique idempotency keys; unique `(payrollRunLineId, channel)` reservations; positive DECIMAL(18,2); approved-parent/source/grant/channel/employee linkage triggers; equality to each full approved allocation; and deferred nonempty batch/sum checks. Multiple batches may use different employees. Prepared obligations are reserved once and cannot be silently deleted or reassigned. An auditable reservation-release/cancellation workflow is not implemented or inferred. Grants and payment lines cannot be updated/deleted through normal SQL; grant revocation/renewal administration requires a separately reviewed workflow (expiry is supported). Payroll history referenced by payment records is protected from mutation by triggers.

Confirmation storage reserves unique batch/idempotency identity, different confirmer, timestamps, channel-specific evidence kind, evidence reference/digest, verification authority, attestation and durable audit snapshot. Confirmation rows are immutable. A later confirmation/state change must commit atomically with version increment. These database rules verify relational/provenance shape, not a physical bank transfer or receipt authenticity. No confirmation operation is exposed or exercised here; a trusted evidence-verification implementation must reject unchecked text before any future confirmation insert. General batch responses omit obligation/profile/evidence JSON and credentials. Privileged administrators can disable triggers; no administrator-proof cryptographic history is claimed.

The existing disposable PostgreSQL harness applies all migrations only to its uniquely generated local database, verifies server/database identity, exercises genuine payroll creation/approval workflows, and tests prepared payment records and rollback-only invalid probes. It does not create confirmations, receipts, transfers or vouchers. Cleanup verifies ownership/OID/marker and zero other sessions. Existing salary-dev business-table counts are checked unchanged. Preparation UI remains read-only and does not call batch persistence automatically.
